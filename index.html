Questions

Does BibliCall tell me what's right or wrong?

No. BibliCall is a comparison and reflection tool. It shows you how different traditions think about a question so you can reach your own conclusion — it never issues a verdict.

Which faiths does it draw from?

BibliCall draws from wisdom traditions and primary texts from around the world, presented side by side without favoring any one over another.

Is this a religious app?

It's built for anyone who wants a wiser, more grounded answer to a hard question — regardless of their own beliefs or background.
